Shoot Out rules
  • One frame, maximum 10 minutes.
  • 15 seconds per shot in the first 5 minutes, 10 seconds in the last 5.
  • Every shot must pot a ball or a ball must hit a cushion — otherwise it's a foul.
  • Every foul gives the opponent ball in hand.
  • Running out of shot clock is a time foul (5 points).
  • The shot clock starts once the balls have stopped — tap it to start; tap the frame clock to stop or restart the frame time.
  • Highest score when time runs out wins. A tie goes to a blue ball shoot-out.
